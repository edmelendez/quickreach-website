For adults & older relatives

For kids & their grown-ups

QUICKREACH KIDS

A first phone's most important button. Kids reach Mom & Dad or a trusted helper, check in when they arrive, and can't change the settings by accident.

  • Mom & Dad: tap a circle to turn one off; one always stays on. Changing their numbers needs the PIN
  • Caregiver or Other helper (you can rename both): when one is on, the Call Button calls them
  • Safe Zones with an I'M SAFE check-in, plus a loud Siren
  • Apple Watch Call Button on the wrist

Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone and Apple Watch.

How it works

From press to call in four steps

Holding the button, instead of tapping it, keeps a phone in a pocket from calling anyone by mistake.

  1. 1

    Press and hold

    Hold the Call Button for 1.5 seconds. A ring fills around it to show how long.

  2. 2

    Hear who's being called

    The app raises the volume and says the person's name out loud.

  3. 3

    The call starts

    It goes to the contact that's on, and the app says their name out loud.

  4. 4

    Text your location

    A help text with the street address and Apple Maps and Google Maps links is ready. Tap Send.
