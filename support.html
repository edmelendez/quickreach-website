Frequently asked questions

Getting started

What do the apps do?

Both apps put the people who care for you behind one big Call Button. Hold it to call, then send a text with your location. QUICKREACH is for adults and older relatives. QUICKREACH KIDS is set up by a parent or guardian on a child's phone.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. Everything you enter stays on the phone.

How do I set up the app?
  1. Choose It's for me or I'm helping someone, and agree to the privacy details. If you set it up for someone else, they need to agree to it.
  2. Create a 4-digit PIN, and send yourself the recovery code by text or email.
  3. Add the phone numbers to call.
Does it cost anything?

No. Both apps are free, with no in-app purchases, ads or subscriptions.

Which languages are supported?

English, Español, Français, Deutsch, Italiano, Português, हिन्दी, 日本語 and Filipino. Change the language with the EN button at the top of the screen.

Calling

How does the Call Button work?
  1. Press and hold the Call Button for 1.5 seconds. A ring fills around it.
  2. The app raises the volume and says who it's calling.
  3. The call starts.
  4. A text with your address and map links is ready. Tap Send in Messages.
Who does QUICKREACH call?QUICKREACH

Your main contact. Change it by tapping LOCKED next to the contact and entering the PIN.

Who does QUICKREACH KIDS call?KIDS
  • Mom & Dad each have a circle. Tap one to turn it off; one of them always stays on.
  • Only one of Other 1 or Other 2 can be on. When one is on, the Call Button calls that helper. You can rename either tile.

To switch helpers, tap the circle on a tile.

Why do I have to hold the button?

Holding for 1.5 seconds stops calls from a quick tap or a phone in a pocket.

Does it work on Apple Watch?

Yes. The Apple Watch app has the Call Button, and you can add it to your watch face. After a call, you can text your location from the watch.

Location

How do I send my location?

After a call, or by tapping the red pin under the Call Button, the app prepares a text with your street address and Apple Maps and Google Maps links. Tap Send in Messages.

Are texts sent automatically?

No. The app writes the text and opens Messages. A person always taps Send.

Does the app track me?

No. Location is read only while the app is open on the screen. It isn't tracked in the background and isn't sent to us.

Why is the address sometimes a little off?

Indoors or with a weak signal, the address can be off by a few houses. The map links use the GPS position, which is usually closer.

QUICKREACH KIDS features

What are Safe Zones and I'M SAFE?KIDS

Safe Zones are places your child goes often: Home, School and Other. When your child arrives, the banner turns green. Tapping I'M SAFE texts the family that they arrived.

How do I add a place?KIDS
  1. Open Safe Zone / Health, tap Edit and enter the PIN.
  2. Choose a place from the nearby list, or search for a place or an address. The search uses Apple Maps.
  3. Tap Use this place, then Save.

To remove a place, tap the red ✕ next to it and enter the PIN.

How does the siren work?KIDS

Hold the logo at the bottom of the screen for 2 seconds. A loud siren plays, even on silent, and the phone vibrates. Tap anywhere to stop. The volume goes back to where it was. The siren is off during setup.

Health ID

What is the Health ID?

A card with what a helper needs to know: name, age, blood type, medications, allergies, conditions, notes and emergency contacts. It can be included in the help text. Tap LOCKED and enter the PIN to change it.

Can it read from Apple Health?

Only if you allow it. The app can fill in date of birth, sex, blood type, wheelchair use and medications from Apple Health. It never writes to Apple Health.

PIN & privacy

What does the PIN protect?

Phone numbers, the Health ID, the language and withdrawing consent all need the PIN. In QUICKREACH KIDS, Safe Zones and turning Mom & Dad off need it too.

I forgot my PIN.

Tap Forgot PIN? and enter the recovery code you sent yourself during setup. Without the code, you can reset the PIN after a 24-hour wait. Entering the right PIN during the wait cancels the reset.

How do I withdraw consent or delete my information?

Tap the ⓘ button at the top of the screen and choose Withdraw consent (needs the PIN). Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the phone. We don't keep a copy. Read the Privacy Policy.
